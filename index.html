What Is Negative Keywords in Google Ads and Why Use Them?

Every business running Google Ads pays for some clicks that bring no value. Negative keywords in Google Ads help you block those unwanted searches, so your budget goes only to people who are really looking for what you offer.

What Are Negative Keywords?

If you are wondering what is negative keywords in google ads, the answer is simple. A negative keyword is a word or phrase that tells Google, “Do not show my ad when someone searches for this.”

Think of it like a filter. When you add a word as a negative keyword, your ad will not appear for any search that contains it. This keeps your ads away from people who are not your target customers.

Why Negative Keywords Matter for Your Ads

Google Ads works on a pay-per-click model. This means you pay each time someone clicks your ad, even if that person never buys anything. Without any filter, a part of your budget can go to the wrong audience.

Save Your Budget

Block searches that bring clicks but no sales, so every rupee or dollar works harder for you.

Reach Better Buyers

Your ads show to people with real interest, which means more enquiries and more sales.

Improve Ad Performance

When more of the right people click, your click-through rate and overall results improve.

Types of Negative Keyword Match

When you use negative keywords in google ads, you can choose how strictly each word is blocked. There are three match types.

Broad Match

Your ad will not show if the search contains all your negative words in any order. It is the default type and gives the widest blocking.

Phrase Match

Your ad will not show if the search contains your exact phrase in the same order. Extra words before or after are allowed.

Exact Match

Your ad will not show only when the search is exactly your negative keyword, with no extra words.

How to Add Negative Keywords in Google Ads

Adding them is easy. Just follow these steps.

  1. Sign in to your Google Ads account and open the campaign you want to improve.
  2. Click on “Keywords” and then choose “Negative keywords” from the menu.
  3. Click the blue plus button and select whether you want to add them to a campaign or an ad group.
  4. Type your negative words, one on each line, and choose the match type you want.
  5. Save your changes. Google will stop showing your ads for those searches.

You can also check the “Search terms” report to see what people actually typed before clicking your ad. Any unwanted term you find there can be added as a negative keyword.

Tips to Get the Best Results

Using negative keywords in google ads is not a one-time job. Searches change every day, so your list should grow with your campaigns. Keep these simple tips in mind.

Check Search Terms Weekly

Review the search terms report often and add new unwanted words as soon as you spot them.

Be Careful With Broad Words

Do not block a word that may also bring good customers. Think twice before adding it.

Use Shared Lists

Create one list of common unwanted words, such as “free” or “jobs,” and apply it to many campaigns at once.
